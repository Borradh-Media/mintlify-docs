The Clinic

One client at a time,
and always the truth

Ms Beauty Clinic is a private, one-room studio in Leixlip — deliberately small. There's no upselling, no rushing you out the door for the next appointment, and no promising results a treatment can't deliver.

Every client is seen personally, from the first consultation through to aftercare. If a treatment isn't right for your skin, you'll be told so — and pointed toward what actually will work.

  • Fully qualified, insured and continually re-certified
  • Professional-grade GiGi and clinical skincare only
  • Free parking and a discreet, private entrance
  • Aftercare advice included with every treatment

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a consultation before booking a treatment?

For advanced treatments such as microneedling or peels, yes — it lets us check your skin is suitable and plan properly. For facials you can book directly.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends entirely on your skin and your goal. You'll be given a realistic number at consultation rather than an open-ended course.

Is there downtime?

Most facials have none. Peels and microneedling can leave the skin pink or flaky for a few days — this is discussed fully beforehand so you can plan around events.

What is your cancellation policy?

CONFIRM: please give 24 hours' notice so the appointment can be offered to someone else. Deposit and cancellation terms to be confirmed by the clinic.

Where are you based and is there parking?

The clinic is in Leixlip, Co. Kildare, with free parking directly outside. The full address is sent with your booking confirmation.
