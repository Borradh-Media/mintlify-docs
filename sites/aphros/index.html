About Aphrós

Experienced hands,
a quiet room

Aphrós is a small independent clinic in Riverchapel, just outside Gorey. It was built around a simple idea: that injury treatment and beauty treatment both deserve proper time, proper training and a room where you're not rushed.

Clients come for a sports injury and stay for the nails — or the other way around. Everything is done by appointment, one client at a time.

  • Qualified and insured in both injury therapy and beauty
  • Private treatment room, appointment only
  • Free parking on site in Riverchapel
  • Serving Gorey, Courtown and north Wexford

Ready when you are

Appointments are booked by phone or message. Tell us what's going on and we'll find you the right slot.
